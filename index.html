Meet your creator

Hi, I'm Kimberly

I've always had a natural love for visual branding and capturing content within my community, the kind of moments we can all look back on. That passion led my career organically into marketing management, where I've done everything from social media to working deep in the data, but being creative is where I thrive. Over 5+ years of working with brands, founders and talent in the health and wellness space, I've learned how to tell a story through content and what makes it truly resonate. Now I bring that same eye to your celebration.

After my move from Hawaii to San Diego, this idea stayed on my mind. I always find myself reaching for the home videos that are a little more raw. Those are the ones I rewatch far more than anything perfectly polished. My ultimate goal is to create modern, luxury content that makes a statement, while leaning into the connection and sentimental moments that make your day so special. Moments you can look back on with nostalgia, not just post on social media.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is a day-of content creator?

I capture your day the way you'd want to see it on your phone: candid, behind-the-scenes iPhone clips and quick social-ready edits, delivered fast. It's not a replacement for your photographer or videographer, it's the piece in between.

Why a vintage camcorder?

The camcorder footage has that warm, home-video feel and can be a private keepsake just for you. That said, the camcorder has a charm all its own, so if you love the look, I'm happy to film with it for your socials too.

How do we receive our content?

All raw footage is delivered within 24 hours through a private gallery link, which stays open for 30 days so you have plenty of time to download everything. Your social-ready edits are added to the same link within 48 hours.

Do you travel?

Yes. I'm based in Southern California and travel throughout California and worldwide. Travel within 50 miles is complimentary, and anything further is quoted during your consultation.

Do you work with a second shooter?

Yes. For larger celebrations of 200+ guests, or events with longer coverage, I can bring on a second shooter to ensure no moments are missed.
